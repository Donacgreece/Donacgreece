## About me

My work sits between IT operations and software development. I design and support infrastructure, networks, Microsoft 365 environments and operational systems, while also creating applications that solve real day-to-day problems.

I am especially interested in monitoring, automation, service continuity, internal tools, user-focused interfaces and platforms that turn complex workflows into clear, reliable experiences.

I also founded and develop [Respawn.gr](https://respawn.gr), an independent gaming publication built around a custom editorial and content management platform.

## Current focus

* Building **Portivo Control Center**, a modern management platform for network switches and bulk infrastructure operations
* Developing operational tools with **Python, Flask, FastAPI and React**
* Creating Microsoft 365 solutions with **SharePoint Online, SPFx and Microsoft Graph**
* Writing bilingual technical articles about real projects, infrastructure and problem solving
